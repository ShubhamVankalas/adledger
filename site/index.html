How it works

Four sources, one ledger. AdLedger stitches an anonymous visitor to a lead and then to a paying customer, across devices.

Step 1 · Click

Pixel

One script tag records landings, UTMs and click IDs like gclid and fbclid in a first-party cookie.

Step 2 · Lead

Forms

Any form on your site, or Typeform, Tally and Webflow through a webhook. Email and phone are hashed.

Step 3 · Cash

Stripe

Payments, renewals and refunds arrive by webhook and are matched to the person who clicked.

Step 4 · Spend

Ad platforms

Meta and Google Ads spend syncs every 6 hours down to the ad, then each sale is credited to its ads.

Everything the paid tools do for the core job.

Hyros, Triple Whale and Cometly charge monthly, often as a share of revenue. AdLedger covers the core attribution job for free, on your own server.

Exact money math

Every amount is stored in whole cents. Split revenue always adds up to the payment, to the cent.

Three attribution models

First touch, last touch and linear. Renewals are credited to the journey that won the customer.

Wasted spend alerts

Campaigns with real spend and ROAS under 0.5x are flagged on the overview and in the weekly note.

Drill down to the ad

Campaign, ad set and ad tables with CPL, CAC and ROAS. Export any view as CSV.

Bring your own AI

Weekly insights from Ollama or LM Studio on your machine, or OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini and OpenRouter. Numbers are computed in SQL, and any number the model invents is flagged.

Nothing hidden

Revenue without a tracked click shows up as unattributed rather than being quietly given to an ad.

Private by default

Raw emails live in one table only. IPs are truncated and API credentials are encrypted at rest.

Demo in one click

Realistic mock connectors for every integration, so you can try everything before connecting a single account.

Running in five minutes.

You need Docker and nothing else. Setup happens in the browser, including connecting Stripe, Meta and Google. There are no config files to edit.

Your computer

Docker Compose

Starts AdLedger and its database. Open localhost:3000 and create your account.

git clone https://github.com/ShubhamVankalas/adledger
cd adledger
docker compose up -d
A server

One-line install

On any VPS. Generates secrets and gets a free HTTPS certificate for your domain.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/\
ShubhamVankalas/adledger/main/install.sh \
  | DOMAIN=ads.yoursite.com sh
Just trying it

Single container

No database to run. Uses the built-in embedded database stored in a volume.

docker run -d -p 3000:3000 \
  -v adledger:/data \
  ghcr.io/shubhamvankalas/adledger

Also deploys to Render, Railway, Coolify, Dokploy and CapRover. Upgrades are docker compose pull && docker compose up -d, and database migrations run automatically.
